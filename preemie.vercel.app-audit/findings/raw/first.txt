Medically reviewed by Dr. Zeeshan Islam, MBBS, MCPS (Pediatrics) Last reviewed August 2026 · Formulas and sources The formula Weeks of prematurity = 40 weeks − gestational age at birth. Corrected age = chronological age − weeks of prematurity. Forty weeks means 280 days. Everything is done in days and converted at the end, which avoids the rounding errors you get from working in whole months. Worked example 1 — 28 weeks, now 6 months old Prematurity: 280 − (28 × 7) = 280 − 196 = 84 days (12 weeks) Chronological age: 6 months = about 183 days Corrected age: 183 − 84 = 99 days = about 3 months 8 days This baby's first birthday cake is six months away, but developmentally you should be reading the 4-month milestone row soon, not the 6-month row. Worked example 2 — 34+3 weeks, now 10 weeks old GA in days: (34 × 7) + 3 = 241 Prematurity: 280 − 241 = 39 days Chronological: 70 days Corrected: 70 − 39 = 31 days, about 4½ weeks Late preterm correction is the one most often skipped.
