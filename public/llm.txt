# AdjustedAge

> Author: Dr. Zeeshan Islam, MBBS, MCPS (Pediatrics), consultant paediatrician.
> Identity card: https://drzeeshanislam.blog (verified accounts and current bio).
> Author's medical-writing practice: https://drzeewrites.com.

## About

AdjustedAge is a single-purpose clinical tool that turns two inputs — date of birth
and gestational age at birth — into every downstream answer in preterm follow-up:
corrected age, chronological age, postmenstrual age, the correct milestone row,
and a serial visit record that persists across encounters.

All calculations use whole days, UTC-normalised dates. No data is sent to any server;
everything runs client-side and persists in localStorage only.

## Key Concepts

### Corrected Age
Corrected age = chronological age − (280 − gestational age in days) / 7 weeks.
Negative before term and displayed as such rather than clamped.

### Postmenstrual Age (PMA)
PMA = gestational age at birth + chronological age. Used for clinical decisions
below 50 weeks.

### Gestational Age Bands
- Extremely preterm: <28 weeks
- Very preterm: 28–32 weeks
- Moderate preterm: 32–34 weeks
- Late preterm: 34–37 weeks

## Safety

- No pass/fail verdict is ever emitted.
- CDC act-early red flags always surface at every age.
- "Contact your paediatrician — don't wait for the next visit" appears on every result.
- Loss of a previously acquired skill is flagged as a same-week concern at any age.
